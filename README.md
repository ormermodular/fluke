# Fluke

*Eight tracks of random CV. Catch what you like and it becomes a loop.*

Fluke is a sibling to Shoal, Ormer Modular's generative melody sequencer, and a lot smaller. It is a CV
generator with eight tracks of ever-changing random voltage. When something sounds right you catch it, and the
last stretch that played becomes a loop. A MUTATE knob then decides how much that loop drifts each time round,
from perfectly still to slowly wandering. Each track has its own style of randomness, plus Chance, Rate,
Direction, Slide and Quantise, with the key set per track or for the whole module.

Everything is on a 12 HP panel with a small screen and a single encoder. Turn the encoder to pick a track, click
and hold to go into its settings, turn to scroll them, click to edit a value, double-click to finish, and click and
hold again to come back out. Every setting is also in the right-click menu.

**[Manual for VCV Rack](https://ormermodular.github.io/fluke/vcv/)** · [`docs/vcv/MANUAL.md`](docs/vcv/MANUAL.md)

## Installing

Fluke for VCV Rack is sold through the VCV Library (Mac, Windows and Linux). Add it to your account on its
Library page, then in Rack choose **Library > Update all** and add Fluke from the module browser, under
Ormer Modular. It needs Rack 2.
